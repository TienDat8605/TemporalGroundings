\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Video Temporal Grounding: Single-Event to One-to-Many}
Video Temporal Grounding (VTG), also known as natural language video moment retrieval, aims to localize temporal boundaries $[s, e]$ in an untrimmed video described by a natural language query~\cite{gao2017tall,anne2017localizing}. Early approaches explored cross-modal proposal ranking~\cite{gao2017tall} and dense anchor regression~\cite{lei2021momentdetr}. The introduction of the QVHighlights benchmark~\cite{lei2021qvhighlights} expanded the field toward joint moment retrieval and highlight detection. However, existing datasets (e.g., Charades-STA~\cite{gao2017tall}, ActivityNet-Captions~\cite{anne2017localizing}) enforce a strict single-instance constraint ($K=1$). To address realistic multi-event occurrences, Xu \emph{et al.}~\cite{omtg2026} proposed \textbf{One-to-Many Temporal Grounding (OMTG)}, revealing that standard video grounders and multimodal LLMs collapse to $0.00\%$ cardinality accuracy. In this work, we present the first training-free, zero-shot framework specifically designed to solve OMTG cardinality collapse.

\subsection{Multimodal Large Language Models for Video Understanding}
Recent advances in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), such as Video-LLaVA~\cite{lin2023videollava}, VTimeLLM~\cite{li2023vtimellm}, Qwen2-VL~\cite{bai2024qwen2vl}, and TimeLens-8B~\cite{timelens2024}, have demonstrated impressive zero-shot temporal comprehension. TimeLens-8B incorporates dedicated temporal coordinate tokens to predict continuous timestamps directly in text. Nonetheless, when processing long untrimmed videos, standard MLLMs ingest visual tokens sampled uniformly across the entire video duration. In OMTG scenarios spanning hundreds of seconds of irrelevant background footage, uniform sampling causes visual token dilution and triggers false-positive hallucinations in empty intervals. Our work demonstrates that restructuring prompt syntax recovers latent multi-span ability, while intelligent candidate corridor extraction eliminates background visual noise.

\subsection{Training-Free and Coarse-to-Fine Video Acceleration}
Efficient long-form video processing has motivated hierarchical coarse-to-fine processing strategies~\cite{lei2021momentdetr,lin2023unitime}. In contrast to approaches that require end-to-end gradient fine-tuning or specialized architectural modifications, training-free inference methods route computational resources adaptively at test time. Adaptive SGDE introduces an ultra-lightweight 1.04B embedding scout operating at a coarse rate of 1.0~FPS to rapidly scan the entire timeline, followed by sub-linear duration-adaptive window geometry ($\Delta(T), G(T)$) and soft continuity merging. This decouples coarse semantic search from fine boundary decoding, slashing expensive 8B Vision Transformer frame evaluations by $60.3\%$ while boosting grounding precision and cardinality accuracy.
