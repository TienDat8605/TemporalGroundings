\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we addressed the multi-event temporal grounding problem in Multimodal Large Language Models. We showed that baseline performance drops stem from prompt format misalignment and uninformative background frames in long videos. To address both challenges without fine-tuning, we presented ScoutTG, a four-stage training-free framework combining temporal scouting at 1.0~FPS, per-video similarity normalization, energy scoring, duration-adaptive windowing, and structured JSON decoding. On the complete OMTG benchmark, ScoutTG achieves 33.12\% Count Accuracy and 19.86\% Effective Temporal F1, while reducing 8B model frame evaluations by 60.3\%. 

\paragraph{Limitations and Future Work.} At 1.0~FPS, our proposal extraction covers 80.3\% of ground-truth action spans, which limits the current system on very brief actions. Future work will investigate adaptive scout sampling rates and in-ViT motion-residual token pruning for scaling to hour-long video comprehension.
