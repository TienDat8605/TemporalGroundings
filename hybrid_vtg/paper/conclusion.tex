\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we addressed the catastrophic cardinality collapse of Multimodal Large Language Models on One-to-Many Video Temporal Grounding (OMTG). We showed that baseline failure stems from two orthogonal sources: conversational prompt format misalignment and background distractor pollution in untrimmed long videos. To overcome both challenges without parameter fine-tuning, we presented \textbf{Adaptive Scout-Guided Dense Evidence (Adaptive SGDE)}, a four-stage training-free framework combining 1.0~FPS temporal scouting, video-adaptive $Z$-score normalization, hysteresis energy mining, sub-linear duration-adaptive geometry, and structured JSON decoding. On the full OMTG benchmark ($N=320$), Adaptive SGDE achieves strong empirical gains ($33.12\%$ Count Accuracy, $19.86\%$ EtF1) while reducing 8B Vision Transformer frame load by $60.3\%$ ($-54.0\%$ net FLOP reduction). 

\paragraph{Limitations and Future Work.} The 1.0~FPS scouting rate establishes an $80.3\%$ candidate coverage ceiling, filtering brief sub-second actions. Future work will investigate adaptive scout sampling rates and in-ViT motion-residual token pruning for scaling to hour-long video comprehension.
