\begin{abstract}
Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) are typically evaluated on single-event retrieval. When evaluated on One-to-Many Video Temporal Grounding (OMTG), where a natural language query corresponds to multiple disjoint intervals in long videos, standard models struggle to detect multiple occurrences. In this paper, we propose ScoutTG, a training-free framework that improves multi-event temporal localization without parameter fine-tuning. We show that baseline performance drops stem largely from prompt formatting issues and uninformative background frames in long videos. By combining structured prompt formatting with a lightweight 1.04B scout and duration-adaptive window planning, our approach removes unnecessary background frames before dense inference. On the OMTG benchmark, ScoutTG achieves 33.12\% Count Accuracy and 19.86\% Effective Temporal F1, while reducing primary 8B model frame evaluations by 60.3\%.

\keywords{Video Temporal Grounding \and Multimodal LLMs \and One-to-Many Grounding \and Semantic Scouting \and Training-Free Inference.}
\end{abstract}
